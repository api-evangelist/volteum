# Volteum

> Volteum is an EV fleet electrification and management software platform. It helps companies plan the transition of vehicle fleets to electric (electrification planning), optimise charging, and monitor mixed fleets live. The platform connects directly to vehicles through OEM APIs (30+ brands) with no hardware installation, tracking mileage, fault codes, charging sessions, maintenance intervals, and costs across electric, diesel, petrol, and hybrid fleets. Volteum is a Techstars portfolio company. It publishes no public developer portal or API documentation; API integration with telematics providers and fleet management systems is offered to customers directly.
